# Review 3: positioning, hierarchy, banners, privacy and logo exploration

Updated October 26, 2026. Source: the owner's second-pass review in this chat.

## Commercial positioning
The core offer is embedded technology leadership with responsibility for execution. The homepage lead now reads: "Technology leadership, embedded in your business. We set the direction, lead the team, and own the execution."

The scope is not inherently too broad for a technology leadership/delivery partner. The problem was treating seven capabilities as seven unrelated commercial offers. The homepage now presents three connected areas: embedded technology leadership, applied AI/automation, and business systems/delivery. Detailed capability pages remain available so a buyer can understand what the team actually does.

Leadership is first and applied AI second. Custom software is last and reframed as AI-enabled custom applications. It supports the wider relationship rather than leading the positioning. No unsupported retainer price, executive credential, or guaranteed outcome was added.

## Owner requests implemented
- All public adapt22 email addresses and mailto links removed, including footer, contact, notices, structured data, and user-facing API errors. Delivery addresses remain private in the server-only contact handler. This reduces direct address harvesting on the website; it does not claim to prevent scraping or spam generally.
- Company spelling corrected to adapt22 LLC with no space between adapt and 22.
- Services, Approach, About, and Contact use full-width photographic banners with readable overlays, smaller than the homepage hero. Four distinct photographs; no homepage ridge photo reused on Services.
- Alternating service tile fills replaced with one consistent reading surface.
- Founder section background matches the existing grayscale image background, with the full arms preserved. Source photograph was not cut out or retouched.
- Header brand/navigation receive restrained hover/press motion, with reduced-motion behavior preserved.
- Internal route links disable Next.js automatic route scrolling; a path-change reset moves new pages to top. Same-page navigation also returns to top. Explicit hash links retain their section behavior.
- Logo study expanded from three to twelve directions, organized into four comparable families. The prior three options remain included. Current website mark stays provisional and unchanged.
